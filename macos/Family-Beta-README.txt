SurStudio Family Beta — Apple silicon Mac

INSTALL
1. Drag SurStudio into the Applications folder.
2. On first launch, Control-click or right-click SurStudio and choose Open.
3. Confirm Open when macOS asks. Later launches work normally.

WHY THE EXTRA OPEN STEP?
This family beta is ad-hoc signed because it has not yet been signed and notarized with an Apple Developer ID. Never disable Gatekeeper globally.

PRIVACY
- Songs, generated files, recordings, scores, and practice history stay on the device unless the user explicitly shares or downloads them.
- SurStudio does not store Messages recipients, contacts, conversations, or message history.
- Score-card images used by the Mac share sheet are temporary and are removed automatically.

LOCAL AI
The shared beta includes the complete karaoke, lyric, practice, scoring, dashboard, recording, and sharing experience. GPU stem separation and MLX transcription remain an optional developer add-on because the current Python/model environment is not portable or approved for redistribution.

REQUIREMENTS
- Apple-silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
- macOS 14 or newer
- Internet connection for YouTube discovery and public lyric matching

Use only media and lyrics you own or are authorized to process.
